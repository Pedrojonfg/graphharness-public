# Practice: ideal gas processes

**Student:** Give me practice on ideal gas processes.

**Teacher:** Here is the first problem.

> **Problem (recall).** An ideal gas expands isothermally. State what happens to its internal energy and where the absorbed heat goes.

**Student:** Internal energy stays constant because T is constant, so all the heat is turned into work.

**Outcome: solved.** Correct on both counts: internal energy is unchanged and the absorbed heat equals the work done.
